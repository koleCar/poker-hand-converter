## 2. What Rail does

Rail converts poker hand histories, replays and shares hands, keeps a library
of your hands with statistics and analysis, teaches (Learn), hosts a forum,
and - in the desktop app - imports hands as you play and shows a heads-up
display (HUD) at poker rooms that allow it. Rail is a tool for studying your
own play. **Rail is not a poker room or a gambling service**: you cannot play,
bet or win money through it.

  ("data mining"); poker rooms forbid it and so do we.

## 6. The desktop app and the HUD

- The HUD works only at poker rooms whose published rules allow third-party
  HUDs, and only within those rules (compliance mode, on by default and not

  the app hides its analysis, solver and chart screens.
- **Poker rooms' rules are theirs and change.** We check them before every
  release, but you are responsible for following the terms of each room you
  play at. We are not responsible for what a room does to your account
  because of software you chose to run, except where the law says otherwise.
- The desktop app keeps your data on your computer; you are responsible for
